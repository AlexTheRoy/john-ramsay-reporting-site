08

Data & files

  • SourceU.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020–2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), tables B01002, B01003, B08201, B11001, B11007, B19013, B25001, B25002, B25003, B25004, B25077. Geography: 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas.
  • Coverage597 populated Washington ZIPs. PO-box-only ZIPs have no resident population and no Census profile; traffic from them resolves to the surrounding ZIP. Median income is published for 549 ZIPs and median home value for 544.
  • Top-codingThe Census caps medians at “$250,000 or more” for income (3 ZIPs) and “$2,000,000 or more” for home value (4 ZIPs); those ZIPs are shown at the cap.
  • GeographyEach ZIP is assigned to the Washington county holding the largest share of its land (2020 ZCTA-to-county relationship file), and the 39 counties are grouped into eight regions. The city column is left blank: no city file was used, so ZIPs are identified by county.
  • LeadsAll 3,046 GOAL leads for this account, Jan 1 to Oct 14, 2026 (Home and Home (LM) homeowner leads, Auto and Auto (New)), counted by ZIP. 1,696 of 1,697 Home leads and 1,343 of 1,349 Auto leads matched a populated Washington ZIP.
  • PrecisionAll values carry ACS margins of error and are strongest for larger ZIPs. Blank means not published, never zero.
  • FilesJohn-Ramsay-WA-ZIP-Data-10-14-26.csv · every ZIP, every column, with the wildfire and earthquake tier from the companion report.
